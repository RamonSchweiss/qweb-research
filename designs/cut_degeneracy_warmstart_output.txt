
Does telling the partitioner where the qubits already are make the move cheaper?

Belongs to designs/design_cut_degeneracy_EN_addendum_1.md, section A1.5,
registered 4 November 2026 before this run. Nothing here was chosen after seeing
data: the two routes, the withdrawal condition, the degenerate subset, the
invalidating condition and the stopping condition are all in A1.5.

Why this run exists. The objective registered in the note itself was vacuous:
if the incumbent partition is already optimal, the nearest optimal partition to
it is itself, and pairwise refinement started there makes no move. That was
withdrawn in A1.4, not confirmed. The non-vacuous case is an incumbent that is
NOT optimal, which the scoping run of 4 November showed is common: spectral
bisection misses the exact optimum on 4 to 13 of 21 graphs.

The objective, as registered : the incumbent is the partition spectral
                               bisection returns. From there, compare a
                               refinement WARM-STARTED at the incumbent against
                               a COLD restart from a random start. The warm
                               route reaches the optimum as often as the cold
                               one does, and at strictly lower migration cost
                               from the incumbent.

Withdrawal, fixed in advance  : if the difference in migration cost between the
                               two routes is smaller than the resolution limit
                               of section 6 of the note, measured the same way,
                               the objective is withdrawn as UNRESOLVED rather
                               than reported either way. If the warm route
                               reaches the optimum LESS often, it is withdrawn
                               outright, because a cheaper route to a worse cut
                               is not the claim.

Degenerate subset, declared   : graphs on which spectral bisection already
                               returns an optimal partition. There the incumbent
                               is optimal, the question collapses into A1.4, and
                               the case contributes nothing. Reported separately,
                               never pooled.

Invalidating condition        : using spectral bisection's own cut as the
                               optimum instead of the enumeration. Enumeration
                               only, to n = 20.

Needs    : numpy, scipy, partition_topology, and the two scripts this one
           builds on, so that no routine is reimplemented and allowed to drift.
Runtime  : a few minutes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Graphs where spectral bisection already returns an optimal
  partition. There the incumbent IS the destination and the
  question collapses into A1.4. Named before the run; reported
  here because a result that is mostly a subset of zeros has to
  say so.

2 - Does the warm route reach the optimum as often?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The warm route is deterministic: on each usable graph it either
  reaches the exact optimum or it does not. The cold rate is the
  share of the twenty independent starts that reach it. If the warm
  route reaches it LESS often, A1.5 withdraws the objective
  outright.

     n   usable   warm reaches   cold per start   warm >= cold
    10        4           0.75             0.56            yes
    12        6           0.50             0.65             NO
    14        9           0.78             0.61            yes
    16       13           0.77             0.57            yes
    18        6           0.50             0.39            yes
    20        9           0.89             0.38            yes


3 - Migration cost from the incumbent, both routes
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Qubits that must move to get from the incumbent to the partition
  the route returns, in ebits. Only graphs where BOTH routes reach
  the exact optimum are counted, because comparing the cost of
  arriving somewhere worse is not the registered comparison.

     n   pairs     warm     cold     diff   resolution      verdict
    10       3     2.00     2.00    +0.00          nan    no spread
    12       3     2.00     2.00    +0.00          nan    no spread
    14       7     2.00     2.81    +0.81         0.00 warm cheaper
    16      10     2.60     4.07    +1.47         1.33 warm cheaper
    18       3     2.67     3.84    +1.18          nan    no spread
    20       8     2.50     4.19    +1.69         0.00 warm cheaper

  'resolution' is the limit of section 6, measured the same way as
  in A1.2: the mean within-family range of the warm figure across
  the three graph seeds. A difference smaller than it is unresolved,
  including where it agrees with the objective.


4 - The verdict on the registered objective
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The warm route reaches the optimum less often than the cold
  one at at least one size. A1.5 withdraws the objective
  OUTRIGHT on that ground: a cheaper route to a worse cut is
  not the claim, and no cost comparison rescues it.
  Cost comparison: 3 of 6 sizes resolve, 0 are unresolved.
  Of those that resolve: 3 warm cheaper, 0 warm dearer.

  Usable graphs per size: n=10: 4, n=12: 6, n=14: 9, n=16: 13, n=18: 6, n=20: 9
  The usable set is what the declared degenerate subset leaves
  behind, and it is small. Where it is very small, the resolution
  limit is wide and the comparison cannot settle anything - which
  is a fact about the instrument, not a result about warm starts.

==============================================================================
  Bounds as registered: two parts, balanced bipartitions, one
  density, the interaction graph as written, enumeration to n = 20
  as the only ground truth, one heuristic family. The incumbent is
  spectral bisection and nothing else; a different single-shot
  heuristic would give a different incumbent and is not tested.
