Control 2  matched-window permutations  (addendum 2, 20 Oct 2026)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The control registered in section 4 came out degenerate: under random
permutations the orbit never closes within p_max, so there was no
cycle to be constant over and no comparison in either direction. That
is recorded in addendum 2 together with this replacement.

Here: for every run whose cycle has d >= 2, the permutation process is
given the SAME per-layer swap budget and the SAME window length W = d,
with the swaps chosen at random instead of by the router. The question
asked of both arms is identical.

Decision rule, fixed in advance: if the control rate lies within the
seed-to-seed scatter of the router rate, constancy carries no
information about routing and every claim resting on it is withdrawn.

H3  is the free orbit cheaper than enforced period one?
-------------------------------------------------------
Enforcing d = 1 buys layer periodicity with a return network every
layer. The free orbit gets periodicity after t layers and keeps it
over d, paying nothing extra for it. So H3 turns on d, not on gate
counts: if d is small the free orbit is cheaper by exactly the
return-swap cost, and if d is large the periodicity is useless.
